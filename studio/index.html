Why GQA matters

Queries keep their full head count while keys and values use fewer heads. The ratio shown above is the theoretical KV-cache reduction at equal width and context.

Matched ablations

AstraLM allocates bridge gate parameters in both residual modes. Switching the bridge off therefore keeps parameter count exactly equal.

KV bytes = 2 × batch × layers × KV heads × context × head dim × dtype bytes
